How an inspection works

From the listing you found to a decision you can trust.

  1. You book

    Tell us about the car and where it is. We confirm a time with you, and with the seller if you like.

  2. We inspect it

    A full computer scan, a road test and a hands-on check from bumper to bumper.

  3. You get the report

    Findings, photos and fault codes, explained in plain English, the same day.

  4. You decide

    Buy it, walk away, or use the repair costs we list to negotiate the price.

What we check

The things a seller won't mention and a quick test drive won't show.

Hidden fault codes

Stored and history codes from every module, including ones recently cleared.

Engine and drivetrain

Misfires, timing, turbo, oil leaks, cooling, transmission behavior.

Brakes and suspension

Pads, rotors, control arms, bushings, shocks and air suspension.

Body and accident signs

Paint depth, panel gaps, repairs, rust and flood damage.

Electrical and safety

Battery, charging, lights, airbags, ABS and driver-assist systems.

Road test

Noises, vibrations, shifting, steering and how the car drives.

Questions

What's the difference between the shop and the seller option?

Both include a full computer scan, a road test and a written report with photos. At our shop ($160) we also put the car on a lift. At the seller ($300) we go to the car, so you don't have to borrow it.

Will the seller let you inspect it?

Most reputable dealers and private sellers do. If a seller refuses an inspection, that is worth knowing too. We can call the seller to set up a time.

How long does it take?

About one to two hours. You get the report the same day.

Do you also fix cars or check a car I already own?

Yes. We've repaired European cars since 1975. Call or text 682-553-6089 for repairs or a diagnostic on your own car.

How far do you travel?

Anywhere in Dallas–Fort Worth. Not sure we reach you? Call or text 682-553-6089. Need a tow instead? We run a 24/7 wrecker service.
