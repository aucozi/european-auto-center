FAQ

Common questions

What's the difference between in-shop and mobile?

Both include a full computer scan, live data, road test and written report. In-shop ($160) adds a full lift inspection of the underside. Mobile ($300) means we come to you — great for cars that won't start, busy schedules, or inspecting a car before you buy it.

Can you inspect a car I'm thinking of buying?

Yes. Book a mobile diagnostic and we'll meet the vehicle at the dealer or private seller. You'll get a report before you hand over any money.

How long does a diagnostic take?

Most diagnostics take 1–2 hours. Intermittent electrical problems can take longer — we'll call you before going beyond the quoted price.

Do I have to get the repairs done with you?

No. The report is yours to keep. If you do choose us, we'll give you an itemized estimate before any work begins.

What areas do you cover for mobile service?

We cover the Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex. Call 817-737-7300 if you're not sure we reach you. Need a tow instead? We also run a 24/7 wrecker service.
